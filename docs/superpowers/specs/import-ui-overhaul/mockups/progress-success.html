Upload progress & completion states

Two approaches for showing upload progress and results within the "sheet" layout. Click your preference.

A

Progress replaces the left panel

When import begins, the left panel switches from file list to a progress list. Each file shows its progress bar + status. The right panel shows the currently-uploading file's lines scrolling past in real time. When done, the progress panel becomes the results summary. No redirect, no new page.

B

Minimal progress bar + auto-redirect

Clicking "Import all" triggers the upload. A single progress bar appears at the top of the sheet with "Importing 3 files...". Per-file details are collapsed into an expandable section. When complete, the sheet gracefully transitions to a success banner with a countdown, then auto-navigates to the main view. Workflow: drop, glance, done.
